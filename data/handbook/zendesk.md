# Zendesk

## What it is
Zendesk is the helpdesk where customer support tickets live. Tickets often include customer names, email addresses and screenshots of their accounts.

## Owner
Kai Walker (IT) administers the Zendesk instance.

## Access levels
- **Read**: view tickets and their history without replying.
- **Write**: reply to tickets, change ticket fields, assign and close tickets. This is what support agents use every day, and Support Agents get it by default.
- **Admin**: manage triggers, macros, ticket fields, user roles and bulk operations such as mass-closing tickets. Admin always counts as high risk.

## Risk tier
Medium, because tickets contain customer personal data.

## Justification expected
Every Zendesk request needs a justification of at least eight words describing the support work you will do.
