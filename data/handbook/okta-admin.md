# Okta Admin Console

## What it is
The Okta Admin Console controls single sign-on (SSO) for every company tool: app integrations, groups, MFA policy and account recovery. Whoever controls it can sign in as anyone, so changes to SSO configuration are tightly controlled.

## Owner
Kai Walker (IT) owns Okta and approves all changes to SSO configuration.

## Access levels
- **Read**: view users, groups, app assignments and system logs.
- **Write**: assign users to groups and apps, and unlock accounts.
- **Admin**: create app integrations, change MFA and sign-on policies, and manage administrators.

## Risk tier
High, at every access level.

## Justification expected
A justification of at least eight words is required, naming the app integration, group change or support task that needs the access.
