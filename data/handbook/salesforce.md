# Salesforce

## What it is
Salesforce is the CRM. It tracks accounts, contacts, opportunities and the sales pipeline, and support escalations are logged against accounts so sales can see them.

## Owner
Kai Walker (IT) administers the Salesforce org.

## Access levels
- **Read**: view accounts, contacts, opportunities and reports. Useful for looking up account owners or contract details.
- **Write**: create and edit records, log activities and update opportunity fields. Sales Ops get write by default.
- **Admin**: change objects, fields, workflows, profiles and integrations. Admin always counts as high risk.

## Risk tier
Medium, because the CRM holds customer contact details and deal values.

## Justification expected
Every Salesforce request needs a justification of at least eight words that explains which records you need and why.
